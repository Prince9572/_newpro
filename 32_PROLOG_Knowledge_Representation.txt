Experiment 32: PROLOG Knowledge Representation Programs

Program 1: Categorize Animal Characteristics

    % Facts
    animal(mammal, dog).
    animal(mammal, cat).
    animal(bird, sparrow).
    animal(fish, shark).
    animal(reptile, snake).

    % Rules
    has_hair(X) :- animal(mammal, X).
    lays_eggs(X) :- animal(bird, X); animal(reptile, X); animal(fish, X).

    % Queries
    % ?- has_hair(dog).
    % ?- lays_eggs(sparrow).

Output: ?- has_hair(dog). true.

?- lays_eggs(sparrow). true.

Program 2: Knowledge Representation – Plant Classification

    % Facts
    plant(flowering, rose).
    plant(flowering, sunflower).
    plant(non_flowering, fern).
    plant(non_flowering, moss).

    % Rules
    needs_pollination(X) :- plant(flowering, X).
    reproduces_by_spores(X) :- plant(non_flowering, X).

    % Queries
    % ?- needs_pollination(rose).
    % ?- reproduces_by_spores(moss).

Output: ?- needs_pollination(rose). true.

?- reproduces_by_spores(moss). true.
